# July 1st Half

## Summary
The project aims to identify constraints for stars enriched by Pair-Instability Supernovae (PISNe). I did a literature review on metal-poor stars and the field of stellar archaeology. Although the search for definitive chemical signatures of PISNe has not yet yielded conclusive results, the field is still relatively young and rapidly evolving with very recent noval papers

## Papers Read
- Bromm, V., & Yoshida, N. (2011). The First Galaxies. Annual Review of Astronomy and Astrophysics, 49, 373–407.

- Karlsson, T., Johnson, J. L., & Bromm, V. (2008). Uncovering the Chemical Signature of the First Stars in the Universe. The Astrophysical Journal, 679, 6–16.

- Takahashi, K., Yoshida, T., & Umeda, H. (2018). Stellar Yields of Rotating First Stars. II. Pair-instability Supernovae and Comparison with Observations. The Astrophysical Journal, 857, 111.

- Aguado, D. S., Salvadori, S., Skúladóttir, Á., Caffau, E., Bonifacio, P., Vanni, I., Gelli, V., Koutsouridou, I., & Amarsi, A. M. (2023). PISN-explorer: Hunting the Descendants of Very Massive First Stars. Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society, 526, 3447–3462.

## Current Direction
Exploring the code from Hartwig et al. 2023 (Machine Learning Detects Multiplicity of the First Stars in Stellar Archaeology Data) to look for possibilities in extending the code for PISNe
